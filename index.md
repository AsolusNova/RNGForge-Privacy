# RNGForge Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 1, 2026**

RNGForge respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the RNGForge mobile application.

## Information RNGForge Collects

RNGForge itself does not require you to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address in order to use the app.

RNGForge uses third-party advertising services that may automatically collect certain information from your device as described below.

## Advertising

RNGForge uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

- Device and advertising identifiers
- IP address
- General device information
- App interactions and advertising interactions
- Diagnostic and performance information
- Approximate location derived from information such as an IP address

This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, depending on your location and privacy choices.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms resources.

## Consent and Privacy Choices

RNGForge uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy and consent choices where required.

Users in regions where consent or additional privacy choices are required may be presented with a privacy message before advertising services are used.

Your available choices may vary depending on your region and applicable privacy laws.

## Third-Party Services

RNGForge uses services provided by Google, including:

- Google AdMob
- Google Mobile Ads SDK
- Google User Messaging Platform

These services are governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

## Data Sharing

RNGForge does not independently sell your personal information.

Information processed through Google advertising services may be shared or processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their respective privacy policies and your applicable consent choices.

## Children's Privacy

RNGForge is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

RNGForge does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services used by RNGForge may process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

## Data Security

RNGForge does not maintain user accounts or a proprietary database containing personal information collected from users.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the security practices and policies of those providers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RNGForge changes or as legal, advertising, or platform requirements change.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
